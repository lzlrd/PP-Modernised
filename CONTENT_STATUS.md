## Left as vanilla on purpose

- Functional textures where a restyle changes nothing or breaks meaning: `effect/dither`, `misc/vignette`, `misc/credits_vignette`, `misc/nausea`, `misc/shadow`, `misc/enchanted_glint_*`, `misc/unknown_pack`, `misc/unknown_server`, `palettes/trim_base` (the trim colour key), the armour trim pattern masks under `trims/entity/`, and `block/lightning_rod_on` (plain white in vanilla).
- GUI icons whose colour carries meaning (accept/reject, warnings, checkmarks, the globe, the bug report icon), the Realms and friends screens, the title easter-egg logo and the panorama overlay.
- Particles where a recolour reads wrong: steam (geyser), sculk, soul, trial spawner detection, vault connection, generic tinted leaves, glow and infested.
- Fonts (`ascii_sga`, `asciillager`, `nonlatin_european`, `accented`): new glyph art would need drawing by hand.
- `painting/dennis` and the creative-only `item/light_00`..`light_15`.
- Cat collars, adult and baby: PPL has neither, so both stay vanilla and match.
